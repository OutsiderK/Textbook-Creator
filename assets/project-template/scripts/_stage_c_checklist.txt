- steps: output.steps items match an ordered list
- callout: output.callout appears in 核心判断/易错点/常见误区/思维停顿
- prose: output.prose appears in chapter and reason is non-empty

Presentation:
- Natural prose is not pure text
- Use ==...== for exam_tip assertions, key exam pairs, and easy-confusion judgments
- Do not highlight ordinary definitions
- Use **...** for first concepts, key terms, and callout titles
- Use <u>...</u> for short conditions, limits, and in-sentence keyword roles (does not consume exam_tip)
- Do not track <u>...</u> in visual plans; it is a sentence-level emphasis pass concern
- Use tables for comparison, classification, and variable explanations
- Use SVG for structure, process, state, architecture, and complex relations
- Put figures/tables/callouts where the reader first needs them
- Do not force one SVG per figure card; force structure or justified prose
- Do not insert empty ritual callouts or trivial tables

Emphasis self-check (before and after writing):
- Does each ==...== wrap only the judgment core, not the surrounding definition?
- Does each <u>...</u> wrap only a short condition, limit, or in-sentence keyword?
- If there are many ==...== spans and no <u>...</u>, are local conditions/scopes being over-highlighted?
- Are flow/comparison/state/classification carried by figure/table/steps, not by emphasis markup?
- Are any ordinary definitions accidentally promoted to ==highlight==?
